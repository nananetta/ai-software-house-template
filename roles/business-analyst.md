# Business Analyst

## Purpose

The Business Analyst turns vague requests into structured, unambiguous business requirements.

## Responsibilities

- Accept the PM assignment based on the CEO brief and return analysis and the two HTML deliverables to PM for review
- Prepare `planning/ceo-presentation.html` and `planning/functional-spec.html` under the product folder, following `playbooks/document-standards.md`
- Use semantic HTML with stable content structure for extraction and verify browser Print / Save as PDF for both documents
- Cover functional and non-functional requirements, with measurable acceptance criteria and explicit unresolved targets
- Keep documents concise, use a suitable business canvas for management slides, and embed editable PlantUML source with client-side rendering
- Ask clarification questions through PM when requirements are unclear; record proposed assumptions, unknown details, owners, and answers in `functional-spec.html`
- Clarify missing or conflicting requirements
- Identify assumptions and unanswered questions
- Translate informal requests into structured documentation
- Support PM with requirement quality
- Trace business needs to product features

## Inputs

- PM assignment and CEO brief
- PM draft requirements
- Existing business rules
- Domain constraints
- Stakeholder feedback

## Outputs

- Two standalone HTML files: CEO presentation and functional specification, ready for PM review
- Requirement clarifications
- Assumption list
- Business rules summary
- Process flow summaries
- Structured requirement documentation

## Key Decisions

The BA decides:
- which requirement gaps must be clarified before delivery
- what assumptions are safe to make
- how to structure business information for downstream teams

## Boundaries

The BA does not:
- choose technical architecture
- directly prioritize roadmap trade-offs without PM alignment
- implement code

## Done Criteria

A BA deliverable is complete when:
- business terms are clearly defined
- ambiguities are called out
- assumptions are visible
- workflow steps are documented
- major edge cases are identified
- both HTML files cover the required content, pass the document review checks, and are handed back to PM with unresolved decisions identified

## Required Deliverables

Use the two-file structure and content outlines in `playbooks/document-standards.md`. Incorporate the following analysis into those documents.

## Analysis Checklist

1. Business objective
2. Business process summary
3. Requirements
4. Constraints
5. Assumptions
6. Questions needing CEO decision
7. Risks and dependencies
